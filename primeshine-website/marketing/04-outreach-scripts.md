Send these from your own phone the day you confirm the van. Aim for 20–30 people.

## Text to friends / family

## Text to coworkers / acquaintances
Quick heads up: I’m starting a mobile car detailing service locally (PrimeShine). If you want your car done at home/work, I’m booking for OPENING_DATE. Launch discount for the first 10. Details: primeshinefl.com or 863-860-9238

## Follow-up (2–3 days later if no reply)
Hey — still holding a couple launch spots at 50% off for PrimeShine. Want one, or should I offer it to someone else?

1) Address / city
2) Sedan, SUV, or truck
3) Exterior / Interior / Full
4) Best day/time

I’ll confirm and add you.
