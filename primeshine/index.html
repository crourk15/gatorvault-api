FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need to provide water or power?

A nearby hose bib and outlet help, and we can self-contain for most driveway jobs. Tell us in the notes if access is tight.

How long does it take?

Exterior is usually 2–3 hours. Interior 3–4. A full Prime Detail runs 5–6 depending on size and condition.

What if it rains?

We move the appointment. Paint work does not happen in a downpour, and we will not leave you with water spots.

Do you detail boats, RVs, or fleets?

Yes — ask for a custom quote. Fleet and multi-car stops get a better per-vehicle rate.
