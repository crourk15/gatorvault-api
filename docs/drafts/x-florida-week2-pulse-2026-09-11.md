HEADER: Florida is 1-0 under Sumrall, and Week 2 is about cleaning the film

The Sumrall era opened loud: Florida 66, FAU 21 on Sept. 5. Highest-scoring Gators opener since 1994. Now the useful updates are the ones that shape Saturday.

Jadan Baugh (#13, Jr., 6-1 / 228) is locked as RB1 and looked like it: 160 yards and three scores on 14 carries. Aaron Philo debuted as QB1 with 275 passing yards and four total touchdowns before Tramell Jones Jr. closed. Vernell Brown III ripped the first Gators score of the year on a 49-yard catch-and-run (6 for 117). Myles Graham stays locked as WILL.

The correction list is real. FAU still piled up 396 yards. Sumrall and Brad White want cleaner pass rush, alignment, and third-down awareness before SEC weeks hit.

Next: Campbell on Saturday, Sept. 12, 5:30 p.m. in the Swamp (SEC Network+). Camels are 2-0. Florida wants 2-0 for the first time since 2021, with sharper defense on the tape.
