<h2>The Big Picture</h2>
<p>Jon Sumrall’s Florida debut is set: <strong>September 5, 2026 · 7:45 PM ET</strong> vs the FAU Owls at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, on SEC Network. The Week 1 board from Sumrall’s Aug 24 presser is no longer a spring projection — Philo is the named starter, the OL has soft locks, and the open fights that still matter are listed below.</p>
<p>This is the Vault’s pre-opener package: depth chart truth, who has separated in camp, how the offense and defense are set up for September, and the season lookouts that will decide whether Year 1 is a floor or a ceiling.</p>

<h2>Week 1 Depth Chart — What’s Locked</h2>
<p><strong>QB:</strong> Aaron Philo (R-So.) is the Week 1 starter vs FAU and is taking most of the practice snaps. Tramell Jones Jr. stays in the room fight — Sumrall was clear this is not forever — with Aidan Warner third.</p>
<p><strong>Offensive line soft locks (Sumrall presser):</strong> Bryce Lovett (LT) – Knijeah Harris (LG) – Harrison Moore (C) – T.J. Shanahan Jr. (RG) – Emeka Ugorji (RT). Continuity is still the ask, but the Week 1 five is named.</p>
<p><strong>Skill clear leans:</strong> Jadan Baugh at RB1; Eric Singleton Jr. at Z; Vernell Brown III at F; Lacota Dippre at Y. Special teams mid-camp look: VB3 on punt return, London Montgomery leading kick return, Patrick Durkin / Alec Clark / Carter Milliron on the kicking unit.</p>

<h2>Still Open — Camp Battles Into Game Week</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>WR (X):</strong> Dallas Wilson leads the lean; Micah Mays Jr. is still pushing.</li>
<li><strong>TE (H):</strong> Mid-camp lean flipped to Luke Harpring; Amir Jackson remains close.</li>
<li><strong>CB:</strong> Ben Hanks III holds the mid-camp lean with J’Vari Flowers next; Cormani McClain locks the other side.</li>
<li><strong>STAR:</strong> Kanye Clark over Alfonso Allen Jr.</li>
<li><strong>FS:</strong> Mid-camp lean flipped DJ Coleman ahead of Cam Dooley.</li>
</ul>
<p>Those five rooms are the “who’s winning grass” list for the final week of camp — not spring heat indexes.</p>

<h2>Offense — Setup for a Strong Year</h2>
<p>Buster Faulkner’s offense opens with a named QB1, a clear workhorse back, and a portal vertical threat at Z in Singleton. Philo’s job vs FAU is rhythm and explosives: FAU’s 2025 secondary forced only three interceptions, and the Owls’ front allowed 200 rush yards per game (Navy gashed them for 397). Baugh early, then chunk shots when FAU loads the box, is the Week 1 script.</p>
<p>The swing for the season is OL cohesion more than star power. Soft locks get Florida through the opener; sustained SEC snaps will decide whether Philo can play on schedule in October.</p>

<h2>Defense — Setup for a Strong Year</h2>
<p>Brad White’s 3-3-5 leans on known centerpieces: Jayden Woods at JACK, Myles Graham at WILL, Jeramiah McCloud / Brendan Bett / LJ McCray across the front, Jaden Robinson at MIKE, Aaron Chiles at SAM, Bryce Thornton at SS. The secondary battles (Hanks/Flowers, Clark/Allen, Coleman/Dooley) are the install report card — STAR and free safety have to play with leverage against spread sets or the hybrid layer stalls.</p>
<p>Week 1 keys vs FAU track that identity: disrupt Zach Veltkamp’s rhythm, set the edge with Woods, and force FAU’s offense off schedule before the Owls’ explosive-run problem becomes Florida’s explosive-pass problem.</p>

<h2>Season Lookouts Beyond FAU</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>QB room honesty.</strong> Philo starts Week 1. Jones Jr. staying in the fight means snap share and October form are still live stories.</li>
<li><strong>OL continuity.</strong> Soft locks are a floor; SEC physicality is the real test after Campbell and Auburn.</li>
<li><strong>3-3-5 install pace.</strong> JACK/STAR/FS clarity by mid-September decides whether the defense plays with leverage or chases.</li>
<li><strong>Explosive offense without turnovers.</strong> Singleton / Wilson / VB3 give Florida vertical answers — FAU is the first stress test for chunk plays vs a soft secondary.</li>
<li><strong>Special teams identity.</strong> Montgomery on kick return and VB3 on punt return are mid-camp leans worth watching for hidden yardage.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Bottom Line</h2>
<p>Florida walks into Sumrall’s debut with a Week 1 starter, a named offensive line, and a defense that already has its JACK and linebacker anchors. The open battles are real but narrow. FAU is the first proof — run the football, protect Philo, and let Woods set the edge. Everything after that is whether camp soft locks survive SEC Saturdays.</p>
