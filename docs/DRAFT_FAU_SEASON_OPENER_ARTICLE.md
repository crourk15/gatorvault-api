## Body

### The Claim
Jon Sumrall already answered the loudest camp question. On Aug 24 he named **Aaron Philo** the Week 1 starter against FAU. Tramell Jones Jr. is still in the room — Sumrall said this isn’t forever — but September 5 is not a QB audition.

The debut that matters is quieter: can the soft-locked five up front keep Philo playing on schedule, and can Brad White’s front get FAU’s offense off rhythm before the Swamp turns into a track meet?

### Why the Line Is the Story
Sumrall’s presser soft-locked **Lovett–Harris–Moore–Shanahan–Ugorji** for the opener, with Lovett at left tackle. Continuity is still the ask — soft lock is not a finished product — but Florida finally has a named five for game one instead of a spring guessing game.

That matters against this opponent. FAU’s 2025 defense allowed **200 rush yards per game** and got gashed in chunk runs (Navy hit them for 397). If Jadan Baugh is the clear RB1 — and he is on the Week 1 board — the opener script writes itself: establish the run, force FAU to load the box, then let Philo take the shots a secondary that forced only **three interceptions** last year is built to give up.

Miss that sequence and the soft lock becomes the story for the wrong reason. Hit it and Philo’s first night in orange and blue looks like a plan, not a portal hope.

### What Camp Actually Settled
Skill clarity is real: Eric Singleton Jr. owns the Z, Vernell Brown III holds the F, Lacota Dippre is the Y. The fights still live are narrower than the spring heat index made them feel — Dallas Wilson leading Micah Mays Jr. at X, Luke Harpring edging Amir Jackson at H, Ben Hanks III ahead of J’Vari Flowers at one corner, Kanye Clark at STAR, DJ Coleman flipped ahead of Cam Dooley at free safety.

Those rooms will get snaps. They shouldn’t steal the debut narrative. The narrative is whether the locked pieces — Philo, Baugh, the OL five, Jayden Woods at JACK, Myles Graham at WILL — play like a staff that finished camp with answers.

### Defense Has to Spoil Rhythm, Not Chase It
White’s 3-3-5 already has its centerpiece language: Woods at JACK, Graham communicating at WILL, McCloud / Bett / McCray eating the front. FAU under Zach Veltkamp is a shotgun-spread outfit that can get explosive when protected. Florida’s Week 1 keys are blunt for a reason — disrupt Veltkamp, set the edge, take chunk shots the other way when the box empties.

If STAR and free safety are still murky in September, the hybrid layer chases. If Woods wins the edge early, Sumrall’s first night looks like identity, not install panic.

### Night One, Then the Season
**FAU — September 5, 7:45 PM ET, Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, SEC Network.** Official look: orange helmet, blue jersey, white pants.

Win the line and the debut is a statement about Year 1 under Sumrall. Survive it ugly and the next questions write themselves — Jones Jr.’s snap share, OL continuity after Campbell and Auburn, whether JACK/STAR ever play with leverage in SEC weeks.

Philo starts. The line decides if that naming was a beginning or just a presser.
