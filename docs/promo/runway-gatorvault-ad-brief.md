## 1. What this ad is

A 15-second premium **app product ad** for Florida Gators football fans.

It is **not** a vault-door movie. It is **not** a recruiting-only spot. It is **not** official University of Florida creative.

It is a dark, electric, swamp-night phone commercial that shows the **real GatorVault app** — Home, Team, FutureCast, Recruiting, Game Week, Live — then ends on the App Store.

Mood: The Swamp at night. Game-day energy. Insider access.  
Feel: ESPN/bleacher-report quality, but private-club — navy glass, orange heat, blue authority.  
Pace: Tight. One idea per cut. No fluff, no comedy skit, no talking-head influencer.

**One-line concept**

> Florida football, unlocked. One vault. The whole program.

**Tagline (on-screen, end card)**

> Florida Football, Unlocked.

**Approved gradients**

- Hero / night sky: `linear-gradient(165deg, #0021A5 0%, #001656 52%, #001A33 100%)`
- Orange fireline: `linear-gradient(90deg, #FA4616 0%, #FF7A45 100%)`
- Momentum meter: `linear-gradient(90deg, #FA4616 0%, #FACC15 100%)`
- Blue glow wash: `linear-gradient(90deg, #0021A5 0%, #001A33 100%)`

**Approved textures (subtle, 10–25% opacity)**

- Stadium-light bokeh (warm white dots on navy)
- Thin swamp mist over the bottom third
- One faint turf stripe at the very bottom of wide frames

**Never use**

- Neon lime, electric purple, hot pink, Tiffany teal
- Generic “AI gold luxury” palettes
- University of Florida interlocking UF, Albert the Alligator, official Florida Gators logo, or any trademarked gator head
- SEC logo, ESPN logo, Nike / Jordan marks, or other school marks except as they already appear inside **real** app screenshots we supply

### Typography

| Role | Face | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wordmark** | Oswald (fallback Arial Narrow) | 700 | `GATOR` + `VAULT`, tracking tight. Orange bar under **VAULT** only (`#FA4616`, ~6px, rounded). |
| **On-screen supers** | Oswald | 700 | All-caps or title case, white on navy. |
| **UI / body** | Source Sans 3 | 400–700 | Phone screens, labels. |
| **Big numbers** | Source Sans 3 ExtraBold **or** Oswald | 800 | Percentages, scores. |

Do not use Impact, Comic Sans, Papyrus, cursive script, or generic “sports jersey” fonts.

### Wordmark + monogram

**Wordmark (primary)**  
`GATOR` `VAULT` as two words, same size, same weight.  
- On navy: both words **white**, orange underline under VAULT.  
- On white: both words **`#0021A5`**, orange underline under VAULT.  
Never recolor, never drop the underline, never stack as “GatorVault” in a different font.

**Monogram (app icon / avatar)**  
Rounded square, 16px radius. Fill `#0021A5`. Stylized **GV** in `#FA4616`.  
Use on the phone’s home-screen icon and the end card. Do not redraw as an alligator.

**On-art brand:** GatorVault  
**Spoken / store / caption:** GatorVault Insider

### UI look (the app must look like the app)

Cards:

- White (`#FFFFFF`) **or** deep card (`#0A1628`)
- 12px corner radius
- 1px border `rgba(0, 33, 165, 0.18)`
- **3px Gator Orange bar along the top edge** of every featured card
- Soft shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`

Phone chrome:

- iPhone 15/16 Pro, black titanium, very thin bezel
- Screen is edge-to-edge GatorVault — swamp navy status bar, white or navy cards
- Bottom tab bar on navy: Home · Recruiting · Team · Live · More
- Active tab = orange icon + orange label; inactive = storm gray
