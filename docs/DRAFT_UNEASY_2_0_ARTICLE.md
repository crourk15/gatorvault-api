# The Uneasy 2-0

By GatorVault Staff · Sep 16, 2026 · 7 min read

Florida is 2-0, 118-24, first time in five years, and Sumrall is uneasy. Saturday they take that to Jordan-Hare.

Florida is 2-0 for the first time in five years, 118 to 24 after two home Saturdays that never got close, and Jon Sumrall has spent more time on the standard than on the scoreboard. He was happy they won. He was not pleased. He said he was a little uneasy with where they are. That is the honest start of this week. The building wants to live in 66-21 and 52-3. The head coach will not.

Saturday at 7 they take that unease to Jordan-Hare, first conference game, first road game, ESPN. Florida has not won in that building since 1999. Auburn already has a one-point win over Baylor in Atlanta, 17-16, Byrum Brown throwing three interceptions and still leaving with it on a keep left. That is the night Florida has not played yet: a Saturday that stayed a Saturday all the way to the end.

The most stable part of Florida's two weeks is Aaron Philo. He has started both games and gone 16-of-21 in both of them, 517 yards, five touchdowns, and one interception Sumrall put on a catchable ball, not on the quarterback. He changed the play at the line when the call was dead. Tuesday, Buster Faulkner called him cool, calm, and collected, and said they would coach him through DJ Durkin's front and the first crowd that will try to take that away. He still does not have a crown. He has two nights that look like he can keep the keys when the night gets loud.

If you want the best player on offense, you still do not need a vote. Jadan Baugh has 296 yards and five scores on 28 carries, 14 each Saturday, a 61-yard run against Florida Atlantic and a 75-yard run late in the third against Campbell, against a long of 26 a year ago. That is the best back in the country for two weeks, and the honest part is the line has not dominated for him. After 52-3 Sumrall said the starting five was not awful and is not where it needs to be. They did not give Baugh the lanes a back like him should have. A guy went the wrong way. Somebody missed a protection. After the half was already 45-3, T.J. Shanahan Jr. wiped a Baugh run that was already a big gain and then added the personal foul that made it 1st-and-25. Roderick Kearney picked up another. Florida took eight flags against Campbell, seven after halftime. Up 45-3 those flags did not change the score. In Jordan-Hare, while the score is still a score, a hold or a personal foul is first down with 25 yards still to go and the drive is dead. If that line does not get Baugh the lanes there, Philo has to throw over the extra bodies, and a flag after the run means that throw never happens. Sumrall already walked off sick of the depth behind Baugh.

The receivers have made Faulkner's calls look right. Vernell Brown III took the Campbell half to 45-3 with the ball in his hands, two punt returns and a catch, 64 and 70 on the returns, after a 6-for-117 night against FAU. Dallas Wilson hit the 40. Eric Singleton Jr., the Auburn transfer, was a problem in the opener until he left and did not come back. Sumrall had him limited Monday and will not go further than questionable. Emmanuel Oyebadejo came out the same way. The room can still win you a Saturday if the ball arrives on time. The Z they counted on in August might not make the trip.

Defense is still where the identity might live, and two nights did not let it start. They have not opened either game with a three-and-out. After Florida Atlantic, Sumrall spent Monday on football IQ: down and distance, when they are speeding you up, when to crowd a receiver and when to give him space. On 3rd-and-short a corner sat nine yards off the ball. That is too far. They took the deep ball away and still let FAU speed the night up. Campbell put the same start on the field, completions down the field and a field goal, 10-3 after one, then Florida held them to those three points the rest of the night. Sumrall told the defensive backs not to get scared of it. At corner you get beat. The snap that matters is the one after. Myles Graham is still the best player on that side of the ball. Jayden Woods is the one who has to keep Byrum Brown in the pocket, because the keep is how Auburn finished Baylor and the short throw is how they moved it. Keshaun Singleton, Jeremiah Koger, and Chas Nimrod caught it short and ran. Sit off the way they sat off in the Swamp and that throw becomes a long run. Brad White called Saturday a fist fight. That is the version of this defense the first two nights never asked them to be.

Monday, Sumrall said these games count twice, overall and conference, and the last two counted once. If he has to get anybody ready, they have the wrong people. He wants sacks and a finish on the quarterback. The Gators cannot beat the Gators. Some of the backups against Campbell, he said, proved why they are not playing. The standard is a Florida Gator standard, and it is not negotiable. Florida scored 0 in the fourth after the backups went in. Auburn will not sit at 45-3.

The honest range for this roster is still 7-5 or 8-4. Two home blowouts do not change that. Saturday will not decide the season. It will decide whether 2-0 was just two nights in the Swamp or the start of the one they have been pointed at since August. For the first time this year they have to find that tone somewhere other than home. Win it and 3-0 is 1-0 in the SEC.
