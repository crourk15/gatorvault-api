## The operation (whole night)

**Offense is one picture.** Shotgun. Same start. Three answers.

1. Hand it to Baugh. Zone. No puller most of the night. He presses the crease. He will cut it back. He finishes through the body. He does not bounce just to bounce. When they want a different run, they change the lead — a puller, a TE surface, motion holding the backside.
2. Throw it now. Flat, slant, screen, shallow. Philo is catch-and-throw. He is not looking for a second answer on those snaps.
3. The boundary shot when they sit. Wilson tracks it. Brown tracks it. Pocket has to be there.

Motion is the tell and the hold. A receiver walks. If they run with him, you have man. If they stay, you have zone. Same start either way.

Play-action / roll is the same run, different answer. They will fake Baugh and move Philo. That is design. When the pocket dies and he runs, that is not design. He slides. He is not a runner.

Pistol showed up once. Under center showed up as a change-up and as the sneak. Those are looks. They are not the offense.

Wideouts have a run job. They stalk. On the screen, Wilson is the first hat and Brown is the ball.

The second group ran the first group’s offense. Gun. Hand it. Same shot.

**Philo:** platform. Rhythm. Needs the pocket. When the interior stunt or the free edge is there, the play is over. Reminds you of a dropback QB who wins on time, not on the move — not a dual-threat tape.

**Baugh:** one-cut, low pads, contact balance, vision for the cutback. That is the back. The offense is built for him.

**Brown III:** the now throw and the ball they go get. Screen, slant, deep track.

**Wilson:** the boundary and the stalk. He is in the run game even when he does not touch it.

**Defense is one picture.** Four down. Help over the top. Corners off. Live with the short throw if you tackle it. The front is the pressure. They do not need to send people to wreck a dropback. Linebackers will walk up and drop — that is the look, not the rush.

FAU’s night is the short throw and the inside run and, late, the QB keep. Florida’s job on those: come down, wrap, set the edge. The keep is the one that needs the outside hat to stay outside.

Mixed safety depth. Some snaps help is deep. Some snaps one walks down. Do not call the night two-high.

Backups kept the same jobs. Screen. Run fit. Front in the backfield.
