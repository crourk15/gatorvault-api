2. Scroll the **exact** play list.  
3. Match spelling to this sheet (Fade vs Fake, Mtn Fk Cross, Drive HB Under, etc.).  
4. If a play is missing, you pulled the formation from the wrong school book — re-add from **Ohio State** or **West Virginia** as listed.
