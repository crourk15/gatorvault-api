# PART B — MONEY SETUPS (PS5)

## B1. Cross Wheels — Gun Tight Doubles (beats ZONE / MATCH)
- Put RB + TE to the WIDE side
- Hot route: Streak the short-side inside slot (usually Square)
- Read: Triangle/wheel side first → then TE/checkdown → then middle sit
Cover 3 / Cover 2: wheel is live for 5–10
Shade down: wait, then hit wheel up the sideline 15–25
Match / Cover 4: void opens for 10–15

## B2. Shot Fake Cross — Gun Tight Doubles (beats MAN / COVER 0)
- RB + TE to WIDE side
- Hot route: Drag the TE
- Throw on the break. Do not hold.
Cover 1: crosser
Cover 2 Man: TE or Circle/B side
Cover 0: TE or quick cross — ball OUT

## B3. Verticals — Gun Trips TE / Flex
- Trips to WIDE side
- Hot routes: RB = Texas (R1 then Texas hot route)
- Optional: outside trips WR = Comeback
Cover 3: quick seam to middle trips first
Zone: yellows sink → Texas; yellows bite → comeback/post
Blitz expected: leave RB blocking instead of Texas

## B4. MTN Y Post — Trips TE Flex
TE sharp post first. RB speed flat is the HOT vs pressure.

## B5. Drive HB Under — Bunch X Nasty
RB speed-in + short corner. Take first open.

## B6. X Cross — Bunch X Nasty
HB crab is the money read. Then cross traffic.

## B7. Crossers — Gun Bunch (Elitee ZONE setup)
- Leave R1 on STOCK crosser (do not hot-route it)
- Streak Square
- Drag X (the X button receiver)
- Return route on Circle
Read: R1 flat first (instant) → return if zones sink → crosser if zones bite
Do NOT use this vs pure man — audible to Drive / Shot Fake Cross / X Cross

## B8. Dash Flood — Gun Bunch
Streak R1, slant X, return Circle. Flood the boundary.

## B9. Cluster Verticals
Drag the TE.
Read: streak → crosser → drag → RB checkdown

## B10. Stretch Alert Bubble — Singleback Bunch TE
Light box = run Stretch. Overload box = throw Bubble.

## B11. HB Draw
Call on obvious pass downs so they stop jumping your routes.

# PART D — BEAT GOOD DEFENSES (PS5)

## Mid Blitz / Nickel Man heat
1. L2 → Triangle → Triangle = ignore the user (oline stops wigging out on the FS)
2. Put receivers to WIDE side, RB opposite
3. Block 6 if they send 6, but keep one HOT (flat/drag/slant)
4. Flip/motion vs walked-down safeties — they often don’t travel
5. After max protect (L2 + Right Stick UP), snap, then flick Right Stick to playmaker-break a deep route vs man

## Cover 0 / Mug
Shot Fake Cross, MTN Y Post flat, Crossers R1 flat, Drive checkdowns
Ball out on the break

## Cover 3
Cross Wheels, Trips seam, Dash Flood

## Cover 2
Cross Wheels wheel, Verticals Texas high-low

## Cover 4 / Match
Cross Wheels, Crossers, Bunch mesh traffic

## Man press
Bunch rubs, Shot Fake Cross, zig/whip/return, TE/RB wheels, HB crab
Throw on the BREAK. Lead away from the defender.
Back-shoulder: hold L2 and aim to the receiver’s back shoulder

## Soft zone / prevent
Draw, Alert Bubble, sit routes — do not force deep
