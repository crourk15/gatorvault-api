# Home Was the Easy Part

By GatorVault Staff · Sep 15, 2026 · 8 min read

Florida opened at home against Florida Atlantic. The final was 66-21. Aaron Philo went 16-of-21 for 275 yards, three touchdowns, and one interception. Jadan Baugh ran 14 times for 160 yards and three scores. Vernell Brown III caught six for 117 and a touchdown. Eric Singleton Jr. hurt his ankle and did not finish. The game never got close.

Monday after that game, Jon Sumrall watched the tape. Defense was football IQ: knowing what the play is asking, the down and distance, when they speed it up, when to crowd a receiver and when to give him space. On 3rd-and-short a corner sat nine yards off the ball. That is too far. They kept FAU from the long passes. They let FAU speed the game up. The interception was not on Philo. It was a catchable ball that should have been a completion. Philo changed the play at the line when he needed to, and led.

Then Campbell came in. The first drive got a field goal. A couple of completions down the field. Sumrall told the defensive backs not to get scared after a catch. At corner you are going to get beat. Next play. The opening drive was still not a three-and-out.

Florida answered. Philo ran it in from the 3. Patrick Durkin hit a 28-yard field goal. After one it was 10-3.

The second quarter did the rest of the first half. Vernell Brown III scored twice on punt returns, 64 and 70. Dallas Wilson had the 40-yard touchdown. Brown scored again as a receiver, 14 yards. Baugh finished it from the 2 with 20 seconds left. Halftime was 45-3. Philo went 16-of-21 again, 242 yards and two scores.

Then the second half started. Sumrall put the backups in. Every time they hit a big play after halftime, another flag came out. T.J. Shanahan Jr. got called on back-to-back plays, a hold that wiped a Baugh run that was already a big gain, then a personal foul. Roderick Kearney picked up another. A hold or a personal foul can shove you into 1st-and-25. That is first down with 25 yards still to go, and the drive is dead. In this league, Sumrall said, you might as well get ready to give the ball back. The Gators cannot beat the Gators. Seven flags after halftime. One in the first half. Three in the whole opener. Up 45-3, those flags do not change the score. If they show up when the game is still close, they are how you lose.

Late in the third Baugh went 75. Last year his longest run was 26 yards. In two weeks he has 296 yards and five scores on 14 carries each Saturday. Campbell never scored again. The Swamp had 52-3.

Second-team and third-team guys got a chance to prove people right or wrong. Some of them proved why they are not playing. Freshmen have been here since January. The standard is a Florida Gator standard, first team or third, and it is not negotiable. He called the depth behind Baugh subpar. A guy went the wrong way on one run. Somebody missed a protection call. The starting offensive line was not awful and is not where it needs to be. They did not give Baugh as many rushing lanes as they should have.

Florida was 2-0 for the first time in five years. Sumrall walked to the locker room unhappy. Happy they got the win. Not pleased with a lot of the rest. Play that second half in conference and they are about to have a real rude awakening. Walk in like that and they will get absolutely embarrassed.

Monday he was still a little uneasy. Better than Saturday. Not gone. When they rolled the depth of the roster into the game, they did not play clean football. He wants them to finish on the quarterback. He wants sacks. These games count twice, he said. Overall record and conference record. The last two weeks counted once. If he has to get anybody ready to play SEC football, they have the wrong people here. What kills you is the run game. If you do not stop the run against them, you have no chance. Jeremiah Cobb and Omar Mabson II are both over 5.5 yards a carry and have 265 combined. Byrum Brown ran for a thousand yards and 14 scores last year. He is physical, playing with confidence, a lot of time in this system. Eric Singleton Jr. and Emmanuel Oyebadejo are out of the boot. Both were very limited Monday. Questionable. Is there a shot? Yes. Is he ready to say they are going to play? No. Philo is confident and not easily rattled. If he plays like he has played through the first part of this year, he will give them a chance to win every week.

Tuesday the coordinators sat. Buster Faulkner said Durkin's defense plays with its hair on fire, plays fast, plays confident. Big challenge on the road. Same preparation every week, no matter who they play. Philo has not started on the road yet. You are not just playing 11 defenders. You have the crowd. He is cool, calm, and collected. They will coach him through it.

Brad White said Auburn's offense already knows the system. A lot of those guys have been in it with Alex Golesh for years. Pace, tempo, skill, a nasty offensive line. Fist fight. Rock fight. Be ready for that.

Florida scored 118 and allowed 24 at home. Auburn does not play those two games. Florida has not won at Auburn since 1999. Ole Miss, Missouri, South Carolina, Texas in Austin, and Georgia in Atlanta on Halloween are still on the card. Two home blowouts do not change those. The honest range for this roster is still 7-5 or 8-4. 2-0 is what you are supposed to be after FAU and Campbell. First 3-0 since 2019 is what Saturday would be.

You can win games the way they have won these two. If they do not stop the run, if they take flags when the game is still close, if they let the crowd get to Philo, Saturday will not be what they desire.

## Takeaways

- Florida is 2-0 because two home games never got close. The Campbell second half did not cost them. The same flags and slow start can cost them at Auburn.
- Seven flags after halftime shoved drives into 1st-and-25. Up 45-3 those flags do not change the score. If they show up when the game is still close, they are how you lose.
- Monday: these games count twice. Stop the run or you have no chance. Philo gives them a chance to win every week if he plays like the first two.
- Tuesday: Philo is cool and calm in his first road start, and the crowd is the extra defender. White called Auburn a fist fight and a rock fight.
- Honest range is still 7-5 or 8-4. Saturday would be the first 3-0 since 2019. Florida has not won at Auburn since 1999.
