# Home Was the Easy Part

By GatorVault Staff · Sep 15, 2026 · 7 min read

Florida is 2-0 because two Saturdays never got close. Saturday at Auburn is the first night a game can stay close long enough for a slow start and a pile of flags to decide it.

After Florida Atlantic, Jon Sumrall had 66 and still spent Monday on a corner sitting nine yards off the ball on 3rd-and-short. They had taken the deep ball and still let FAU speed it up. The interception was a catchable ball. Eric Singleton Jr. did not finish.

Campbell's first series put that same corner on the field in public, completions down the field and a field goal, the start Sumrall told the defensive backs not to get scared of because at corner you get beat and the snap that matters is the one after. By the time Vernell Brown III was done with the half, it was 45-3 and the field goal no longer mattered.

A lead like that will eat seven flags after halftime and still feel like a win. T.J. Shanahan Jr. wiped a Baugh run and then added the personal foul that made it 1st-and-25, Roderick Kearney in the same half, and nobody in the building had to live with any of it. Byrum Brown and Jeremiah Cobb will. They will play those flags while the score is still a score.

Sumrall was already taking that to Jordan-Hare when Jadan Baugh went 75 late in the third, 296 yards and five scores in two weeks against a long of 26 a year ago, and he still walked off 52-3 unhappy. A guy went the wrong way. The depth behind Baugh was subpar. The line did not give him lanes. The next backfield is Cobb and Omar Mabson II over 5.5 a carry, Byrum a thousand and 14 last year, and if Florida plays that second half in conference they get embarrassed.

Monday, the rooms still had that walk. These games count twice, Sumrall said, overall and conference, and the last two counted once. If Sumrall has to get anybody ready, they have the wrong people. He wants sacks and a finish on the quarterback because Byrum will make you pay if you do not get there. Singleton and Emmanuel Oyebadejo came out of the boot limited, and Sumrall will call both questionable and will not go further than that. Aaron Philo has been clean in this building, 16-of-21 both weeks, a chance to win every week if he stays who he has been, and Saturday is the first crowd that will try to take that away.

If that night stays close, Tuesday belonged to Buster Faulkner and Brad White, the two men who have to call the version of this team that 45-3 never asked to play. Faulkner has to keep a quarterback who has only started here clean when DJ Durkin's front is playing with its hair on fire and the crowd is trying to take Philo with it. He said Philo is cool, calm, and collected, and they will coach him through it. White has to take a defense that just spent two weeks in Gainesville and put it in Alex Golesh's pace and a nasty line, the fist fight he told them to be ready for, the same fight the flags already showed they have not won.

They have not won in that building since 1999. Lose the close one this week and the year that still has Ole Miss, Missouri, South Carolina, Texas, and Georgia in Atlanta in it starts 7-5 or 8-4 from the first night it was allowed to be a fight. 3-0 they have not had since 2019 only lives if Saturday never becomes one.

Home was the easy part. Saturday is the first night they can lose, and the year will not give that night back.

## Takeaways

- Florida is 2-0 because two Saturdays never got close. Saturday is the first night a slow start and a pile of flags can decide a game that stays close.
- Seven flags after halftime did not cost them at 45-3. Byrum Brown and Jeremiah Cobb will play those flags while the score is still a score.
- Sumrall walked off 52-3 already carrying the next backfield.
- Philo has been clean in this building, 16-of-21 both weeks. Saturday is the first crowd that will try to take that away.
- A close loss this week is how a 7-5 or 8-4 year starts. 3-0 since 2019 only lives if Saturday never becomes a fight.
