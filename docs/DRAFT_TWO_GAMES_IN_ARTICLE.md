# Home Was the Easy Part

By GatorVault Staff · Sep 15, 2026 · 7 min read

Florida is 2-0 because two Saturdays never got close. Saturday at Auburn is the first night a close game can take the year with it.

After Florida Atlantic, Jon Sumrall had 66, Eric Singleton Jr. did not finish, and Monday still belonged to a corner sitting nine yards off the ball on 3rd-and-short. Campbell walked that same corner onto the field on the first series. Sumrall had already told the defensive backs not to get scared of it, because at corner you get beat and the snap that matters is the one after, and by the time Vernell Brown III was done with the half it was 45-3.

Seven flags after halftime, T.J. Shanahan Jr. wiping a Baugh run and then the personal foul that made it 1st-and-25, and Byrum Brown and Jeremiah Cobb waiting for a score that is still a score. Sumrall was already taking that to Jordan-Hare when Jadan Baugh went 75 late in the third against a long of 26 a year ago and still walked off 52-3 unhappy with what is behind Baugh, Cobb and Omar Mabson II over 5.5 a carry and Byrum a thousand and 14 last year already in the next backfield. Play that second half in conference and they get embarrassed.

Monday, the rooms still had that walk, and these games count twice, Sumrall said, overall and conference, the last two counted once. If Sumrall has to get anybody ready, they have the wrong people. He wants sacks and a finish on the quarterback because Byrum will make you pay if you do not get there, and Aaron Philo has been clean in this building into DJ Durkin's hair-on-fire front, Buster Faulkner saying they will coach him through the noise, Brad White putting that same defense into Alex Golesh's pace and the fist fight he told them to be ready for.

Singleton and Emmanuel Oyebadejo came out of the boot limited, and Sumrall will call both questionable and will not go further than that. They have not won at Auburn since 1999, and a close loss this week is how a year that still has Ole Miss and Georgia in it starts 7-5 or 8-4.

Home was the easy part. Lose Saturday and the year will not remember they were ever 2-0.
