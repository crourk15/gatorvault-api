# Home Was the Easy Part

By GatorVault Staff · Sep 15, 2026 · 7 min read

Florida is 2-0 because two Saturdays never got close. Saturday at Auburn is the first night they can lose.

After Florida Atlantic, Jon Sumrall had 66 and spent Monday on a corner sitting nine yards off the ball on 3rd-and-short, the same corner Campbell walked onto the field with on the first series, completions and a field goal, the start he told the defensive backs not to get scared of because at corner you get beat and the snap that matters is the one after. Eric Singleton Jr. did not finish. By the time Vernell Brown III was done with the half it was 45-3.

A lead like that will eat seven flags after halftime, T.J. Shanahan Jr. wiping a Baugh run and then the personal foul that made it 1st-and-25, and still feel like a win. Byrum Brown and Jeremiah Cobb will play those flags while the score is still a score, and Sumrall was already taking that to Jordan-Hare when Jadan Baugh went 75 late in the third against a long of 26 a year ago and still walked off 52-3 unhappy, a guy the wrong way, the depth behind Baugh subpar, the line without lanes, Cobb and Omar Mabson II over 5.5 a carry and Byrum a thousand and 14 last year already waiting. Play that second half in conference and they get embarrassed.

Monday, the rooms still had that walk. These games count twice, Sumrall said, overall and conference, and the last two counted once. If Sumrall has to get anybody ready, they have the wrong people. He wants sacks and a finish on the quarterback because Byrum will make you pay if you do not get there, and Aaron Philo has been clean in this building, a chance to win every week if he stays who he has been, into DJ Durkin's hair-on-fire front and the first crowd that will try to take that away. Singleton and Emmanuel Oyebadejo came out of the boot limited, and Sumrall will call both questionable and will not go further than that.

Tuesday, Buster Faulkner and Brad White have to call it if the night stays close, Faulkner keeping a quarterback who has only started here clean in that noise, White putting a defense that just lived in Gainesville into Alex Golesh's pace and a nasty line, the fist fight he told them to be ready for. They have not won in that building since 1999. A close loss this week is how a year that still has Ole Miss and Georgia in it starts 7-5 or 8-4.

Home was the easy part. Saturday is the first night that can take the year.
