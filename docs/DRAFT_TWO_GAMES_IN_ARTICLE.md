# Home Was the Easy Part

By GatorVault Staff · Sep 15, 2026 · 7 min read

Florida is 2-0 because two Saturdays never got close. That is the whole of it, and the limit of it. Saturday at Auburn is the first night a game can stay close long enough for a slow start and a pile of flags to decide it.

Jon Sumrall had 66 on Florida Atlantic, Aaron Philo's 16-of-21 and Jadan Baugh's 160 included, and still spent Monday on a corner sitting nine yards off the ball on 3rd-and-short, on a defense that took the deep ball and let FAU speed it up, and on a catchable ball that became the interception. Eric Singleton Jr. did not finish.

Campbell's first series was that corner in public, completions down the field and a field goal, the start Sumrall told the defensive backs not to get scared of because at corner you get beat and the snap that matters is the one after. Then Vernell Brown III ended any chance the night would stay a game, 45-3 at the half, Philo 16-of-21 again for 242 and two, Campbell's field goal already a memory.

A lead like that will eat seven flags after halftime, T.J. Shanahan Jr. wiping a Baugh run and then a personal foul, Roderick Kearney, 1st-and-25, and still feel like a win. Byrum Brown and Jeremiah Cobb will not give them a lead that fat. Sumrall was already taking that to Jordan-Hare when Baugh went 75 late in the third, 296 yards and five scores in two weeks against a long of 26 a year ago.

He walked off 52-3 unhappy because a guy went the wrong way, the depth behind Baugh was subpar, the line did not give him lanes, and the next backfield is Cobb and Omar Mabson II over 5.5 a carry with Byrum having run for a thousand and 14 last year. Play that second half in conference and they get embarrassed. Year One in this building treats 52-3 as unfinished.

Monday the rooms still had that walk. These games count twice, Sumrall said, overall and conference, and the last two counted once. If he has to get anybody ready, they have the wrong people. He wants sacks and a finish on the quarterback. Singleton and Emmanuel Oyebadejo came out of the boot, limited, questionable, no further than that. Philo has been clean in this building, a chance to win every week if he stays who he has been, and Saturday is the first crowd that will try to take that away.

If the game stays close, Tuesday belonged to Buster Faulkner and Brad White. Faulkner takes a quarterback who has only started here into DJ Durkin's hair-on-fire front plus the noise and said Philo is cool, calm, and collected, and they will coach him through it. White takes a defense that just lived in Gainesville and puts it in Alex Golesh's pace, a nasty line, the fist fight he told them to be ready for.

They have not won in that building since 1999, and Ole Miss, Missouri, South Carolina, Texas, and Georgia in Atlanta will treat a close loss as the real beginning of the year, the 7-5 or 8-4 kind, not a 3-0 they have not had since 2019.

Home was the easy part. Saturday is the first night 2-0 can die in public, and the rest of the card will not resurrect it.

## Takeaways

- Florida is 2-0 because two Saturdays never got close. Saturday is the first night a game can stay close long enough for the start and the flags to decide it.
- Seven flags after halftime did not cost them at 45-3. Byrum Brown and Jeremiah Cobb will not give them a lead that fat.
- Sumrall walked off 52-3 already carrying the next backfield.
- Philo has been clean in this building. Saturday is the first crowd that will try to take that away.
- A close loss is the 7-5 or 8-4 year. 3-0 since 2019 only happens if Saturday stays the way the first two stayed.
