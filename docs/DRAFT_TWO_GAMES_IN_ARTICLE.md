# Home Was the Easy Part

By GatorVault Staff · Sep 15, 2026 · 8 min read

Florida is 2-0 because two Saturdays never got close. That is the whole of it, and the limit of it. Home was the easy part. Saturday at Auburn is the first night that can make 2-0 look like two games.

Florida Atlantic left 66-21, and the building took it as proof. Aaron Philo went 16-of-21 for 275 yards, three touchdowns, and one interception. Jadan Baugh ran 14 times for 160 and three. Vernell Brown III caught six for 117. Eric Singleton Jr. left on an ankle. Jon Sumrall spent the next Monday on a corner sitting nine yards off the ball on 3rd-and-short, on a defense that took away the long pass and still let FAU speed the game up, on a catchable interception that was not on Philo. A 66-point night that sends the coach back to football IQ is not a test. It is evidence that the score can lie.

Campbell was supposed to bury that. Instead the first series put it on the field: completions down the field, a field goal, the start Sumrall told the defensive backs not to get scared of. At corner you are going to get beat, and the snap that matters is the one after. Then Vernell Brown III took the night, Dallas Wilson hit the 40, Baugh finished it from the 2, and it was 45-3 with Philo at 242 yards and two scores. The opening never had to live. That is not a half. That is a disguise.

Because it was 45-3, the second half could get sloppy and still feel like a win. Sumrall put the backups in and the flags came with them, T.J. Shanahan Jr. wiping a Baugh run that was already a big gain and then a personal foul, Roderick Kearney right behind him, 1st-and-25, the drive already dead. Seven after halftime. One in the first half. Three in the opener. Byrum Brown and Jeremiah Cobb do not play 45-3. If those flags show up while the score is still a score, they are how you lose, and Sumrall was already walking that toward Jordan-Hare before Baugh even ran the 75 late in the third.

The 75 was leftover. Last year Baugh's longest was 26. In two weeks he has 296 yards and five scores on 14 carries each Saturday. Campbell never got off three. They posted 52-3 and the first 2-0 start in five years, and Sumrall went to the locker room unhappy with the same number in his pocket, happy they won and not pleased with the rest of it, because the depth behind Baugh was subpar, a guy went the wrong way, somebody missed a protection, and the starting line was not awful and is not where it needs to be. Year One in this building is a coach deciding 52-3 is not the point. Play that second half in conference and they will get embarrassed. The team that can do it is already waiting, Cobb and Omar Mabson II over 5.5 yards a carry and 265 combined, Byrum Brown a thousand yards and 14 scores last year, physical, time in the system, the run that kills you if you do not stop it.

Monday, that walk-off was still in the rooms. It had cooled, and it had not left. These games count twice, Sumrall said, overall and conference, and the last two weeks counted once. If he has to get anybody ready for SEC football, they have the wrong people here. He wants sacks. He wants them to finish on the quarterback. Singleton and Oyebadejo came out of the boot for a limited Monday and are questionable, which is as far as Sumrall would go. Philo has been the one thing that did not need a disguise, confident, not easily rattled, a chance to win every week if he is who he has been. He has only done it with this crowd behind him. Saturday it has to hold in a building that will not help him.

If that close game is real, Tuesday belonged to the men who have to call it. Buster Faulkner has to keep a quarterback who has only started here clean when DJ Durkin's defense is playing with its hair on fire, fast and confident, eleven defenders plus a crowd. He said Philo is cool, calm, and collected, and they will coach him through it. Brad White has to take a defense that just spent two weeks in the Swamp and put it in Alex Golesh's offense, pace and tempo and a nasty line, the fist fight he told them to be ready for.

Florida scored 118 and allowed 24 in two home games that never got close. Auburn does not owe them a third. Florida has not won in that building since 1999. Ole Miss, Missouri, South Carolina, Texas in Austin, and Georgia in Atlanta on Halloween are still waiting. The honest range is still 7-5 or 8-4. First 3-0 since 2019 is only what Saturday would be if the night stays easy, and there is no reason to believe it will.

Home was the easy part. The first close game is the first real one, and it is this week.

## Takeaways

- Florida is 2-0 because two home games never got close. That is evidence, not a test.
- The Campbell flags did not cost them at 45-3. The same flags can cost them at Auburn if the game is still close.
- Sumrall walked off 52-3 already carrying the run game that can embarrass them.
- Philo has been enough here. Saturday is the first time the crowd is not his.
- Honest range is still 7-5 or 8-4. Saturday would be the first 3-0 since 2019. Florida has not won at Auburn since 1999.
