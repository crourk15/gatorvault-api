# Product Summary

GatorVault delivers:

- FutureCast Movement Intel
- Recruiting Hub (commits, targets, portal)
- Film Room (scheme, breakdowns, pressers)
- GatorNation Live (real-time feed)
- Game Week (matchups, win probability)
- NIL Tracker (SEC rankings, UF KPIs)
- Insider Articles
- Personalized alerts and watchlists

The GameDay Dashboard is the centerpiece — a live, breathing, ESPN-style experience.
